Company Profile

High-performance window and door systems shaped by connected production.

e-gooo is the premium window and door brand of Liaoning Hengxin Aluminum Co., Ltd., a company founded in 2011. Established in 2015, the brand focuses on high-end aluminum system windows, doors and sunroom solutions for private residences and project-based applications.

Guided by a philosophy of quiet living, e-gooo uses minimalist design and system engineering to create a refined acoustic, thermal and spatial experience. Its R&D model integrates cloud-based product development, industrial data, digital simulation and 3D printing to support full lifecycle product management.

2011 / 2015

Company foundation and brand launch

Liaoning Hengxin Aluminum Co., Ltd. was founded in 2011; e-gooo was launched in 2015 as its premium Windows & Doors brand.
