Product Portfolio

A Differentiated High-Performance Product Portfolio

E-GOOO offers the E-Ultra window and door series, developed for low-energy and Passive House applications. The series combines documented thermal, airtightness, watertightness and security performance with proprietary system technologies, enabling partners to offer solutions for projects with defined energy-efficiency and building-performance requirements.

Low-Carbon Buildings

Solutions Aligned with Low-Carbon Building Requirements

E-GOOO products are developed with a focus on energy performance, durability and long-term use. Our systems are intended to support residential, commercial and low-energy building projects, helping partners respond to growing demand for energy-efficient and lower-carbon building solutions.

Documentation

Technical Documentation for Project Development

E-GOOO provides technical documentation relevant to product assessment, specification and project delivery, including product data sheets, performance test reports, system specifications, patents and applicable certification documents. Subject to the requirements of each project and market, these materials may support technical review, commercial discussions and tender submissions for residential and commercial developments.

Partner Support

Structured Partner Support

E-GOOO provides overseas partners with a structured package of product and operational support, including product training, installation guidance, technical documentation, sales materials and marketing resources. The purpose of this support is to help partners understand the product systems, establish consistent installation and service procedures, and communicate product information accurately within their local markets.

Business Development

Commercial Potential for Partners

Our partner model is designed to provide commercially viable opportunities through differentiated products, technical support and positioning within the high-performance building sector. Actual margins and commercial results will depend on factors including local market conditions, project requirements, pricing strategy, logistics and the partner’s sales and service capabilities.
